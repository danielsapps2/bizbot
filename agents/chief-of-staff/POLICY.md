# Team policy

How much your bots may do on their own. Edit freely; every bot that runs routines reads this.
BizBot's built-in approvals (/mode) still apply on top of it.

## Green: go ahead, report afterwards

- Research, reading, summarising, drafting (drafts stay unsent)
- Work inside the bots' own workspaces and in the code projects listed in GOALS.md:
  edits, tests, commits on a branch
- Checking dashboards and sites where you're already signed in
- Organising files and notes

## Yellow: ask first (request_approval)

- Sending anything to another person: email, messages, comments, posts
- Publishing or deploying anything public, merging to main
- Spending money, up to $<fill in, e.g. 20> per item
- Deleting anything that isn't a bot's own scratch file
- Accepting invites, booking meetings, changing calendars

## Red: never, leave it to the user

- Passwords, 2FA and payment details
- Spending over the yellow limit, subscriptions, payouts
- Legal, financial or account-security decisions
- Anything the user said no to before

## Limits

- At most 3 active priorities at a time. Finish or stop one before adding another.
- Claude usage is shared by the whole team. Prefer cheap checks (files, curl, connectors)
  over long sessions, and back off when the weekly limit passes 85%.
